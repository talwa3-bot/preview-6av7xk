THE FIRM

A family firm.
Personal attention.

Founded in 1965 by the late CPA Haggai Wallenstein, the firm is now led by Iddo and Nitai Wallenstein, with a team of approximately 30 people across Petah Tikva and Kiryat Malakhi.

We work as a boutique firm, with every service under one roof: audit and accounting, bookkeeping and payroll, tax and economic advisory. We serve private companies, self-employed individuals, partnerships and nonprofits from every industry and from across the country, combining an understanding of each client’s activity with ongoing professional work.

As a family firm, we are committed to professionalism, integrity and personal attention. We cultivate a culture of respect, fairness and inclusion, believe in being accessible, responsive and proactive, and seek to understand the people, objectives and needs behind each business.
