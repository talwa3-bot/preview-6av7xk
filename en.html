About us

Behind every financial decision is a human story.

Our firm was founded by CPA Haggai Wallenstein, of blessed memory. More than60 years later, the family firm continues to operate as a boutique practice, providing professional, high-quality and reliable accounting and related services to a wide range of clients, all under one roof.

The firm is led by the next generation, Iddo and Nitai Wallenstein. The team numbers about30 employees, who work with a commitment to professionalism, integrity and values focused on the human side, no less than the business side. The firm has offices in Petah Tikva and in Kiryat Malachi.

At Haggai Wallenstein & Co. we aim to connect with our clients on a personal level, and to understand their goals, concerns and aspirations. Availability from partner level down is a commitment we keep every day.

Today our firm works with the most advanced software on the market, including artificial intelligence tools and automation, payroll at the highest level, economic analyses, budget vs. actual, and cash flow.

An IT team and a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) are responsible for securing and safeguarding the firm's information.
