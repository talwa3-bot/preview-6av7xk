THE FIRM

The firm

Founded by the late CPA Haggai Wallenstein, the firm is now led by Iddo and Nitai Wallenstein, with a team of approximately 30 people across Petah Tikva and Kiryat Malakhi.

Our work covers audit and accounting, bookkeeping and payroll, tax and economic advisory. We serve private companies, self-employed individuals, partnerships and nonprofits across industries, combining an understanding of each client’s activity with ongoing professional work.

As a family firm, we are committed to professionalism, integrity and personal attention. We cultivate respect and fairness within our team and seek to understand the people, objectives and needs behind each business.
